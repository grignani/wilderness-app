Privacy Policy

Wilderness for iOS

Wilderness is built with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is stored, and what controls you have.

What data does Wilderness collect?

Wilderness collects two types of data, both stored locally on your device:

  • Location data — GPS coordinates used to determine which map regions you have visited and to record your travel path.
  • Motion data — Activity type (walking, running, driving, stationary) used solely to adjust GPS precision and reduce battery drain. Motion data is not stored.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, your exploration data is optionally backed up to your personal iCloud account so it can be restored if you reinstall the app. No data is ever sent to external servers.

Does Wilderness share my data?

No. Your data is never shared with third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no server-side components. Wilderness does not communicate with any external service.

What about accounts and tracking?

Wilderness does not require or support user accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no way to identify you. The app contains no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, and no usage analytics.

How can I manage my data?

  • Pause tracking — Disable location collection at any time from Settings.
  • Export data — Export your exploration data as JSON from Settings.
  • Delete data — Permanently delete all data from both your device and iCloud backup from Settings.
  • Revoke permissions — Revoke location or motion permissions from iOS Settings at any time.

Background location

With your permission, Wilderness tracks your location in the background so you can explore without keeping the app open. You can disable background tracking in Settings. The blue status bar indicator is shown when background tracking is active.

Children's privacy

Wilderness does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. The app does not collect contact information, so there is no way to notify you directly — check back here if you have concerns.
